# BerCom Website — Image Sourcing Guide

Exactly what to search for on Pinterest (or any stock site) for each image slot, with
size/orientation so they drop in cleanly. Once downloaded: **Admin → Media Library →
Upload**, then paste the resulting `/uploads/...` URL into the field noted below
(Admin → Pages → [page] → edit the section → paste into the image field, using
"Browse Library" to copy the URL).

Keep the same subject matter throughout — offshore oil & gas / industrial / QHSE —
so the site reads as one consistent brand, not a mix of stock styles.

---

## Home Page

**Hero slides** (Admin → Pages → Home → Hero Carousel section) — 3 slides, each needs
one large photo, **landscape, 1600×1000px or larger**:
- Pinterest search: `"offshore oil platform sunset"`, `"offshore support vessel ocean"`,
  `"oil rig aerial photography"`
- Mood: dramatic, moody lighting (dawn/dusk works well against the dark text panel),
  real vessels/platforms — not power-line/pylon photos (current placeholders are
  generic energy stock images, not oil & gas specific)

**"BerCom in the Field" gallery** (Home → Image Gallery section) — 4+ photos,
**landscape, 1200×800px**:
- Pinterest search: `"offshore worker PPE"`, `"ship deck operations"`,
  `"oil and gas logistics port"`, `"industrial safety training"`

## About Page

**"Our Story" image** (About → Text With Image section) — 1 photo, **portrait or
square, 800×800 to 800×1000px**:
- Pinterest search: `"oil gas team safety gear"`, `"offshore crew group photo"`

**"Our People, Our Operations" gallery** (About → Image Gallery section) — 4 photos,
**square, 800×800px**:

## Testimonials Page

**Client headshots** (Admin → Collections → Testimonials → edit each of the 6) —
**square, 400×400px, professional headshot, neutral background**:
- Pinterest search: `"professional headshot business"` — pick photos matching each
  author's implied gender/seniority (e.g. "Operations Manager" → business-casual
  portrait)
- ⚠️ These are illustrative headshots standing in for named client quotes, not
  photos of the actual people — pick clearly generic/stock-style headshots, not
  identifiable real individuals, to avoid implying a false endorsement.

---

## General tips

- Stay in landscape/square orientations matching the sizes above — portrait photos
  get awkwardly cropped by the `object-fit: cover` styling used everywhere.
- Compress before uploading (TinyPNG or similar) — the Media Library has an 8MB
  per-file limit and large unoptimized photos slow the site down.
- Consistent color grading (the current placeholders lean blue/teal/dusk-orange)
  will make the new photos feel like one shoot rather than a stock-photo grab-bag.
