Catalyst Handling & Mechanical Services

In partnership with HPA, BerCom Services delivers world-class catalyst handling for CPP owners and FPSO operators — highly trained personnel, specialised offshore-compliant equipment, and 30+ years of proven expertise.

compress
Dense Loading — HYDROPAC®

Maximum catalyst loading with uniform flow distribution. The proprietary HYDROPAC® system achieves optimal density — the world leader in dense loading technology.

  • ±3% density accuracy vs. theoretical maximum
  • Reversible rotation — loads around transfer tubes
  • Positioned 6" below distributor tray — loads higher
  • No centre-shaft obstruction for full bed uniformity
  • Beds do not sag or alter flow during the run
tune
Reformer Loading — UNIPAC

Fast, high-quality tube loading for reformers. Substantially reduces loading time while eliminating hot spots, reducing settling and prolonging tube life.

  • Accommodates 3–10" internal diameter tubes
  • No pre-sacking required
  • No vibration required during loading
  • Eliminates hot spots for longer tube life
  • Significantly faster than conventional methods
engineering
